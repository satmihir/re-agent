### Permanent inverse proof

`approved_exec_ledger_updates` first requires equality of the entire old/new case lock except `ledger_sha256`, then verifies complete frozen raw replay and recomputes the actual case lock. It checks every exact added row/count and the absence of the old restoration rows. Its three mask bits mean: omit the identified cache rows, omit the identified relative trace rows, restore the identified full-history rows. All eight masks are enumerated with unchanged certificates and canonical-sorted sites. **Exactly one must reconstruct the complete old case hash, and it must be mask 7**: the complete relocation, never a partial mask. A case outside the 32, normalized change, extra/missing/wrong-count site, changed certificate, forged replay or other mask still fails the ordinary earlier-area guard. The implementation is platform-independent; it does not grant a fixed Linux digest exemption to Darwin.
